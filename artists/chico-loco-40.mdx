**Chico Loco 40** (CL40 World) is the stage name of **Samir Libari**, a moroccan-american rapper, songwriter and music producer. He is the founder of the label [CL40 World](/labels/cl40-world) and records with the production studio [WR Beats](/labels/wr-beats).

## Biography

Chico Loco 40 began writing lyrics in 2010, after the death of his father, (M'Hamed Libari), He started his artistic journey in 2014 alongside his brother, **LB0025** (Abdelghafour Libari).

He returned to music in 2024 and developed the CL40 project with the producer alias QUARANTA-FOUR-ZERO and the studio WR Beats. In 2026 he co-founded the label CL40 World with his brother.

His music was first distributed through DistroKid, then through Symphonic Distribution and UnitedMasters.

## Sound

His sound is dark, atmospheric and cinematic. It blends underground rap and alternative music with the energy of drill and trap, and features lyrics in Spanish and English. Recurring themes include street reality, resilience, survival and social awareness.
