**WR Beats** (WR Beats Studio) is a music production and engineering studio and beats marketplace. It powers the releases of the label [CL40 World](/labels/cl40-world) and of the artist [Chico Loco 40](/artists/chico-loco-40).

## About

WR Beats handles production and engineering for the CL40 catalog. Chico Loco 40 has worked with the studio since his return to music in 2024. The studio also offers beats and instrumentals, including UK drill and trap compilations published by CL40 World.
