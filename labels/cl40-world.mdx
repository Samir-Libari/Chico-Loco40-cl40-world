**CL40 World** (CL40 World LLC) is an International record label and music company. It was founded by the rapper [Chico Loco 40](/artists/chico-loco-40) (Samir Libari) from United States and his brother **LB0025** (Abdelghafour Libari), with operations between New York City and Tangier, Morocco.

## About

CL40 World launched its international operations in 2026. It releases the catalog of Chico Loco 40 and LB0025, produced and engineered at WR Beats studio. Beyond releases, CL40 World runs an online store for instrumentals, merchandise and digital assets.
