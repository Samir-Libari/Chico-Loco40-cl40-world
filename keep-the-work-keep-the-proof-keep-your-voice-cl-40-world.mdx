CORE IDENTITY Type: Global creator-defense and media network, presented in this campaign as a mercenary company whose paid contracts are protection, investigation, secure archiving, and reputation defense rather than armed combat Alignment: Chaotic Evil in the campaign's factional shorthand: fiercely self-directed, willing to ruin an opponent's influence, and convinced that institutions protect the powerful. This is a fictional game-world interpretation, not a claim about any real person or company. Size: Huge: approximately 1,200 active members, contributors, associates, and contractors across multiple regions Influence Level: Global, with influence concentrated in online creative communities, music production, digital publishing, and creator-support networks Wealth Level: Variable and uneven: substantial reach and useful infrastructure, but much of its value is invested in studios, software, archives, and member projects rather than freely available cash.

LEADERSHIP Name: Samir Libari Title: Chief Architect of CL40 World Visionary, exacting, resilient, and deeply invested in dignity and independence. His intensity inspires members but can make disagreement feel like disloyalty. Samir Libari is the real-world name supplied for the creative brief and is adapted here as the fictional faction's founder and principal. Within the setting, he builds CL40 World as a connected home for music, publishing, media, technology, and digital communities. The character's campaign actions and magical or mercenary capabilities are invented for play and should not be read as claims about the real person. SYMBOLISM A red square enclosing a white open eye above a blue horizon line. The eye represents watchfulness, the horizon represents a network without borders, and the square recalls a screen or framed work. Colors: Signal red, Midnight blue, Warm white Symbols: The open eye inside a red square, A blue horizon line, A duplicated file mark used by Proofkeepers
